# Image prompts: service and solution pages

Each of the 15 service pages takes **one photograph**, and Evolve and each solution page take
**two**: 25 in all. Everything else on these pages (the product screens, devices, sites and
mockups) is drawn in code and needs no image.

1. **The point-of-view photograph** (every page): a documentary photo beside the page's big
   statement, showing the page's example business.
2. **The panel background** (`<page>-stage`, Evolve and solutions only): a dark, moody photograph
   that sits behind the product panel under the page's opening, at about 45% opacity, with the
   screens and phones on top. The service pages open on a drawn picture instead, so they don't
   need one.

Until a photo arrives, the panel shows its dark gradient in the page's colour and the
point-of-view section shows a quiet placeholder. Each page picks up its photos on its own.

## How to deliver them

1. **Generate each image at 3:2 landscape**, at least 1600 × 1067 px (2400 × 1600 is better).
2. **Name each file exactly as the file name below**, with any image extension, for example
   `whatsapp-automation-stage.jpg`. The names are the SEO file names, so please don't rename them.
3. **Put them all in one folder and give me the path.** I'll crop them to 3:2 and convert each to
   AVIF and WebP at 800 and 1600 px wide, into `public/photos/`.

You can send them in batches.

## The look

**Every photo**

- Photorealistic, like a real documentary photograph. Indian people and settings, Bangalore where
  it fits. Everyday clothes, nothing staged.
- Soft contrast, shallow depth of field, a 35–50 mm lens feel.
- Each page has its own colour (below). Let it show in the light, a wall, fabric or an object —
  never as a filter over everything.
- **Never:** text on the image, readable words on screens or signs, logos or brand names, watermarks,
  posed handshakes, people pointing at charts, or anyone looking into the camera. Screens are soft
  and out of focus.

**Panel backgrounds (`-stage`)**

- Low-key and dark: at least two-thirds of the frame in deep shadow, one or two sources of light.
- No people in focus; empty spaces, objects and light. The left half and the lower right stay
  especially dark, where the screens and phones sit.
- The page's colour in the light itself.

**Point-of-view photos**

- Natural window or evening light, real work in progress, slightly warm and slightly muted.
- Leave calm space in the frame; the photo sits in a rounded panel and the edges crop a little.

Every prompt below already includes this style, so each can be pasted on its own.
