# Statements across the site

Every line written for the twenty service, solution and Evolve pages, gathered in one place so you can review them and ask for changes. Each page lists its search title and description, the opening (H1 and intro line), the product panel's tagline and toast, the point-of-view statement, the figures, every feature title, the comparison line, the section headings and the closing.

Where a line is written in the page content files, the file is named, so any change is one edit:

- Search title, description, H1 intro line and closing: `src/content/pages.ts`
- Everything else on the page: `src/content/products/<page>.ts`

Figures inside the product screens are sample data and are not listed here.
